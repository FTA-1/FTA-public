Forex Trading Academy — Privacy Policy & Terms

Effective date: 12 November 2026

1. What this app is

Forex Trading Academy ("the app") is an educational platform teaching forex mechanics, risk management, journaling discipline and chart analysis. It provides educational decision-support tools only and never executes trades or gives personalised financial advice.

2. Data stored on your device(s)

Guest mode works fully offline. Lesson progress, quiz and exam scores, points and journal entries are stored locally on your device until you choose cloud sync.

3. Data we collect (cloud accounts only)

If you create an account we collect: your full name, email address, account user ID, learning progress (lessons, quiz/exam scores, points) and the trade-journal entries you choose to sync (pair, direction, prices, P/L, notes, timestamps). We include no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs and no tracking identifiers.

4. Who processes it

Data is hosted and processed on our behalf by Supabase (authentication and database). Paid tiers (Pro Monthly, Ultimate Pro, Lifetime) are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing; we never see card details. Optional live signal dashboards load public market data from Binance public endpoints (mirrors) with Bybit fallback; no personal data is sent to those feeds.

5. What we never do

We never sell, share or rent your data. No ads, no analytics, no third-party marketing.

6. Your journal & audit trail

Journal edits require a written reason and always preserve the original entry. If you share an exported journal, you control what leaves your device.

7. Account & data deletion

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from inside the app (Settings → Delete my account) or by emailing fta.support.2026@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Deletion completes within 72 hours and is irreversible. Full details, including what is deleted and any retention: https://fta-1.github.io/FTA-public/delete-account.html

8. Children

The app is not directed to persons under 18 and contains financial-education content intended for adults.

9. Risk disclosure & terms of use

Trading foreign exchange, crypto and metals carries substantial risk; industry data shows 70-80% of retail accounts lose money. All signals, sizes and plans shown are educational illustrations, not financial advice. By using the app you accept these terms.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced inside the app before they take effect.
