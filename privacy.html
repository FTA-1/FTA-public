Forex Trading Academy — Privacy Policy & Terms

Effective date: 18 August 2026

1. What this app is

Forex Trading Academy ("the app") is an educational platform teaching forex mechanics, risk management, journaling and trading psychology. It does not provide financial advice, execute trades, manage capital, or guarantee profits.

2. Data stored on your device

The app works fully offline. All your data — lesson progress, quiz and exam scores, journal entries, edit history, statistics and subscription test flags — is stored only in your device's local storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it (CSV) or enable an optional cloud sync introduced in a future version.

3. Data we collect

Currently none. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs and no tracking identifiers. If cloud accounts are introduced later, this policy will be updated and explicit consent will be required before any data syncs.

4. Subscriptions & payments

Paid tiers (Pro Monthly, Ultimate Pro, Lifetime) are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. We receive only your subscription status (active tier) needed to unlock features. Payment details never touch our servers.

5. Market data & signal dashboards (Ultimate tier)

The optional live signal dashboards load public price data directly from Binance's public API (BTC/USDT, and PAXG/USDT as a gold-tracking proxy) inside a webview. These are automated educational strategy outputs, not financial advice. Telegram alert settings (bot token and chat ID) are stored only in your browser's local storage and are sent only to Telegram's API.

6. Your journal & audit trail

Journal edits require a written reason and always preserve the original entry. If you share an exported journal (for example with a prop firm), you share it voluntarily; the app never transmits it.

7. Children

The app is not directed to persons under 18 and contains financial-education content intended for adults.

8. Risk disclosure & terms of use

Trading foreign exchange, crypto and metals carries substantial risk; industry data shows 70–80% of retail accounts lose money. By using the app you accept full responsibility for your trades, risk management and results. Lessons, statistics and signals are for educational purposes only and are not investment advice. Past performance never guarantees future results.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced inside the app before they take effect.
