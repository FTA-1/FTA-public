Forex Trading Academy — Account & Data Deletion

Forex Trading Academy (developer: Gerrie Wasserman · contact: fta.support.2026@gmail.com) respects your right to delete your account and your data at any time, without penalty.

How to delete your account and all associated data

  1. Send an email from the address registered to your account to fta.support.2026@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
  2. We verify ownership (reply-confirm from the same address) and complete deletion within 72 hours.
  3. An in-app "Delete my account" button in Settings is arriving in an upcoming release; the email route above always works, before and after.

What is deleted

  • Account credentials (email + password hash) and your user ID.
  • Your profile row: full name, subscription tier, expiry date.
  • All cloud-synced trade journal entries (pairs, prices, P/L, notes, timestamps, edit history).
  • Cloud-stored learning progress (lessons, quizzes, exams, points, badges).

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing for advertising or analytics — we collect neither.
  • Deletion is executed directly in the database; the current hosting plan keeps no restorable backups, so removed rows are unrecoverable immediately.
  • Payment records for purchases are retained by Google Play (the payment processor) as required by tax and accounting law — not by us.

Deleting specific data without deleting your account

Email the same address with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and specify exactly what to remove (for example: journal entries by trade reference number). We action verified requests within 72 hours.

Guest / offline mode: data stored only on your device never leaves it. Use Settings → RESET PROGRESS in the app to erase local data instantly.
