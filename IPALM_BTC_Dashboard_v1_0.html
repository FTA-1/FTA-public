Exact rules implemented
  1. H1 bias: latest completed H1 close breaks a confirmed 2-left/2-right swing; price must not have closed back beyond the latest opposite swing.
  2. M15 sweep: wick pierces a confirmed swing, then a completed M15 candle closes back across it within three M15 candles.
  3. Displacement: body ≥ 1.0 × ATR(14), body ≥ 60% of candle range, close in directional outer 25%, and close breaks a confirmed internal swing.
  4. FVG: three-candle gap aligned with displacement; minimum width 0.05 × M15 ATR; expires after eight completed M15 candles and is invalidated by a close through its far edge.
  5. Entry: after price returns into the FVG, wait for a completed M5 candle to trade into it and close in the trade direction beyond its midpoint. Enter only at/near the next candle; otherwise skip.
  6. Stop: beyond the M15 sweep extreme plus 0.10 × M15 ATR. Target is 2R from planned entry. Skip if a confirmed opposing M15 swing leaves less than 2R of room.
  7. One open BTCUSD trade at a time; max three trades/day; stop after two consecutive losses; daily loss stop 1.5% of start-of-day equity. Track these manually: this browser dashboard cannot see your Exness positions or account P/L.
Educational decision-support only, not an automated trading bot. Data feed: Binance BTC/USDT, not Exness BTCUSD. Check the Exness live quote, spread, contract specification, margin and order volume before every trade. If no complete setup is detected, no trade is the correct result.
