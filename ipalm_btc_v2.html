Exact rules implemented (v2.0)

  • C1 regime: M5 ATR(14) between 0.6× and 3.0× its 96-candle median; outside = no trade.
  • C2 depth: ≥200 M5, ≥200 M15, ≥100 H1 completed candles loaded.
  • C3 context: H1 EMA50 vs EMA200 side; counter-trend setups require room ≥2.5R and displacement body ≥1.0×ATR.
  • G1 sweep: M5 wick pierces a confirmed M15 2-left/2-right swing, then a completed M5 candle closes back across within four M5 candles.
  • G2 displacement: body ≥0.8×M5 ATR(14), body ≥50% of range, close in directional outer third, and close breaks a confirmed M5 internal swing.
  • G3 FVG: three-candle gap aligned with displacement; width ≥0.04×M5 ATR; expires after twelve completed M5 candles; invalidated by a close through its far edge.
  • G4 confirmation: completed M5 trades into the FVG and closes beyond its midpoint in trade direction; entry at next candle open, window two candles, otherwise skip.
  • G5 conviction: confirmation close beyond prior close AND candle volume ≥1.2× 20-candle average volume.
  • G6 geometry: stop beyond sweep extreme plus 0.08×M5 ATR; target 2R; skip if room to opposing M15 swing below 2R (2.5R counter-trend).
  • G7 discipline: one open BTCUSD trade at a time; max three trades/day; stop after two consecutive losses; daily loss stop 1.5% of start-of-day equity. Track these manually: this browser dashboard cannot see your Exness positions or account P/L.
